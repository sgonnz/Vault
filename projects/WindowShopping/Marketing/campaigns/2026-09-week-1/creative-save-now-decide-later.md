# WS-202609-004: Save now, decide later

## Purpose

Position saving as a practical alternative to impulse buying. This is a calm, product-light introduction to WindowShopping's central promise.

- Audience: budget-conscious waiters and intentional shoppers.
- Core claim: users can save products, organize them, and set a target price.
- CTA: Build a wishlist you can come back to.
- Destination: `https://windowshoppings.com/` with the placement-specific UTM. Do not launch until the website correction is deployed and the live landing page and store links are verified.
- Status: Draft. Human review required before scheduling.

## Demo and production rules

Use only owned, licensed, or clearly synthetic imagery. This demo uses one synthetic product: a charcoal carryall bag. Do not show a retailer name, a sale, a price drop, an alert, or a guarantee that a target price will be reached.

Because this placement uses a target price, include this exact social qualification in the video and every platform variant: "Monitoring is available for supported products while your browser and device are available."

## Vertical video script and shot list

Format: 9:16, 16 to 20 seconds. The first shot can be a real hand-held phone-style close-up of a laptop if the browser content is synthetic. Use warm, quiet music with documented platform clearance, or no music. Burn captions into the video.

| Time | Visual and shot | Voiceover | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00-0:03 | Close-up of a laptop with the synthetic charcoal bag product page open. Cursor pauses instead of clicking a checkout button. | "I liked it. I just did not need to buy it today." | "Liked it. Did not need it today." |
| 0:03-0:07 | Open WindowShopping and select `Everyday carry` as the destination collection. | "So I made a place for it instead of talking myself into it." | "Choose: Everyday carry" |
| 0:07-0:11 | Before saving, add the note `Compare sizes before buying`, set a personal target price, then click Save product. | "I can keep the details and make a plan." | "Add a note"<br>"Set a target"<br>"Save product" |
| 0:11-0:15 | Show the `Everyday carry` collection beside other clearly synthetic product cards. | "When I am ready, I know where to find it." | "Come back when you are ready" |
| 0:15-0:19 | Simple branded end card with browser extension icon and collection background. | "Save it now. Decide when the price is right." | "Save it now. Decide when the price is right."<br>"Build a better wishlist" |

Editing notes: the feeling is thoughtful, not restrictive. Show the target-price field as planning, not an alert configuration or promised future savings. Keep the collection clean and the cursor movements deliberate.

Required end-card text: "Monitoring is available for supported products while your browser and device are available."

## Accessibility guidance

- Provide burned-in captions that match every spoken line. Do not make the emotional message understandable only from the paused checkout screen.
- Use high-contrast text. Avoid placing copy over the synthetic bag's dark surfaces without a solid overlay.
- Add platform alt text where supported: "A shopper saves a demo charcoal bag to a WindowShopping collection, adds a note, and sets a personal target price."
- Narrate the note and target-price actions because their small interface text may be hard to read on mobile.
- Keep any audio optional. The full message must work silently.
