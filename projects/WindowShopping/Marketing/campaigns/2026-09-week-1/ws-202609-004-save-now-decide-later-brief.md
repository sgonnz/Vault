## Audience moment

The shopper likes an item but does not need to buy immediately. Tabs and screenshots turn a deliberate decision into a lost link or an impulse purchase.

## Single message

Save a product now, then decide when the price is right for you.

## Evidence and claim boundaries

| Claim used | Supporting source and date | Required qualification |

| Users can save products and optionally set a target price. | [Product claims](../../Product%20claims.md), 2026-09-06 | Do not promise that a price will drop, that a target will be reached, or a specific saving. |
