# WS-202609-007: Three-step product demo

## Purpose

Demonstrate the extension's basic save flow with one accurate sequence: open WindowShopping, choose a collection, then save the product.

- Audience: shoppers who want a simple way to keep products from multiple stores in one place.
- Core claim: users can save products from almost any supported HTTPS store and organize them in collections.
- CTA: Add WindowShopping to your browser and save your next find.
- Destination: `https://windowshoppings.com/` with the placement-specific UTM. Do not launch until the website correction is deployed and the live landing page and store links are verified.
- Status: Draft. Human review required before scheduling.

## Demo and production rules

Use a browser-profile demo account and a clearly synthetic product page for a cobalt insulated bottle. The page must visibly say `Demo product` and use no retailer logo. Do not show a notification, restock state, price alert, monitoring status, sales language, or checkout.

The phrase "almost any supported HTTPS store" is accurate but too long for the main voiceover. It is included in the platform captions. If a final cut says or shows anything about monitoring, add the short qualification: "Monitoring is available for supported products while your browser and device are available."

## Vertical video script and shot list

Format: 9:16, 12 to 16 seconds. Record at a readable scale, with no speed-up faster than a viewer can follow. Burn captions into the video.

| Time | Visual and shot | Voiceover | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00-0:02 | Synthetic cobalt bottle product page, with `Demo product` visible. | "See something you want to remember?" | "Found a product you want to keep?" |
| 0:02-0:05 | Click the WindowShopping extension icon. | "One: open WindowShopping." | "1. Open WindowShopping" |
| 0:05-0:08 | Select `Weekend hiking` as the destination collection. | "Two: choose where it belongs." | "2. Choose a collection" |
| 0:08-0:11 | Confirm the capture and click Save product. | "Three: save the product." | "3. Save the product" |
| 0:11-0:15 | Show the saved bottle in the `Weekend hiking` collection, then end card. | "Now it is there when you are ready." | "Saved to Weekend hiking"<br>"Add WindowShopping to your browser" |

Editing notes: the save state must be genuinely produced by the extension. If the capture fields need correction, show the edit screen rather than pretending capture is perfect.

- Add platform alt text where supported: "A three-step screen recording shows a demo cobalt bottle being saved with WindowShopping and added to a Weekend hiking collection."

- The video must show only synthetic or otherwise rights-cleared product imagery. Do not show a real retailer's page or brand logo.
