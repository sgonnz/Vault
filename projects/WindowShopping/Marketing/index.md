## Source of truth

Marketing copy must remain consistent with shipped product behavior, release evidence, the extension listing, privacy disclosures, and then the product website, in that order. The iOS app is pre-release and must not be marketed until [[Product claims]] records a compiled and tested build plus a live App Store URL. If a feature, platform, retailer support level, or alert behavior changes, update [[Product claims]] before creating new content.

No account connection or publishing permission is implied by these notes. Drafts require review before publication.
