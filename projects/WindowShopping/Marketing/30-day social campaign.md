# 30-day social campaign

## Goal

Validate the clearest acquisition message for WindowShopping: saving a find now and deciding later. Use TikTok and Instagram Reels for short demonstrations. Adapt the strongest ideas into Pinterest Pins for ongoing discovery.

Success signals: completed video views, saves, profile visits, link clicks, extension installs, and first saved product. Do not optimize for follower count alone.

## Operating rules

- Treat the 30 rows below as an idea bank, not a daily publishing quota. Select three core concepts each week and create one suitable placement per channel from each concept, for up to nine placements per week.
- Before scheduling, assign each selected concept a date, content ID, owner, operator, destination, and publishing mode (`Automatic` or `Notify Me`) in its content brief.
- Use a controlled demo catalog with owned or licensed imagery, or clearly labeled synthetic products. Do not show a fabricated alert as a real customer event.
- Include the short monitoring qualification in captions or on-screen text whenever price or restock alerts are featured.
- Review every draft for [[Product claims]] before scheduling.

## Weekly review

At the end of each week, identify the top two posts by saves and qualified clicks. Reuse their hook and format in the following week, but test a new audience context such as gifts, home, wardrobe, or a hobby. Record the conclusion, not just the metric: for example, "before-and-after tab cleanup earned saves, while monitoring explanations earned qualified clicks."
